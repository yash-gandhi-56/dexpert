How It Works

Five steps. One tool. Done.

1

Choose your starting point

Pick a pre-built recipe (e.g. APAC investor deck) or start from scratch. Recipes remember your preferred slide sequences.

2

Build, add, and order your slides

Add monthly performance slides, chapter slides, leadership articles, registration slides — all from the master data source, in any order.

3

Attach your custom slides

Have slides unique to you or your team? Drop them in alongside the master content. Everything in one final deck.

4

Export your ready deck

Download a polished, compliance-ready presentation in seconds — exactly as it would have taken hours before.
