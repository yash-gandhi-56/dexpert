Why goals, not labels

Nobody allocates to an "asset class." They allocate to a job that needs doing.

The bond market lists tens of thousands of individual securities across currencies, coupons, credit tiers and maturities. An advisor doesn't need another taxonomy chart for that — they need one clear answer: which building block does which job in a client's book? Ballast. Income. Extra return. A fixed date. Somewhere to park cash before the next decision.

That's how the Xtrackers fixed income range is organised on the pages that follow.

Job 04 — A known date

Target a Maturity

"I need to know exactly when this money comes back — like holding a single bond, without losing the diversification of a fund."

Each Target Maturity ETF holds a diversified, ESG‑screened basket of EUR investment‑grade corporate bonds maturing in the same twelve‑month window. Quarterly coupons flow through as they're collected; capital returns at the stated date, then the fund closes — four rungs an advisor can combine into a laddered portfolio with staggered redemption dates.
